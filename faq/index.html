The Model

Traditional planning models (day-of, partial, and full-service) are built for couples who want to be closely involved in every decision. There’s nothing wrong with that. Higher Love was designed specifically for the couple who is overwhelmed, short on time, or simply doesn’t want wedding planning to be their part-time job.

It’s a fixed planning formula with published pricing and pre-curated options at every turn, including eight complete decor collections available to every planning client, with linens, place settings, glassware, candles, florals, and a matching signage suite. Design is included in every package, and the collections mean you never have to start from scratch. The goal is to minimize decision fatigue and help you feel as calm during planning as you will on your wedding day.

As much or as little as you’d like. If there’s a part of the planning process you’re excited about (florals, music, the aesthetic), let’s collaborate on it. If you’d rather hand it off entirely, I’ll take it from there and only check in when a decision actually needs to be made. The goal is to meet you where you are, not force you into a mold.

No more than two a month, with at least two weeks between wedding dates, so every couple gets my full attention. I don’t pack the calendar, and my rates reflect the level of service, time, and attention you get because of it.

Budget & Fit

A realistic starting budget for a Portland wedding with 50–75 guests is $20,000, and that’s a lean, well-planned event with mid-range vendors and minimal florals. The average Portland wedding runs $28,000–$35,000. Magazine-worthy tablescapes, lush florals, a live band, and a stunning venue put you at $40,000–$60,000+. For Hood River and Gorge weddings, the floor is closer to $24,000.

For vendor-by-vendor ranges, from venue and catering to bar service, photography, and gratuity, see my Portland wedding budget guide. For how bar service and bartender pricing work, see the bar section of the guide.

Higher Love works exclusively with licensed, insured, and experienced vendors, because your wedding day deserves nothing less. The minimums in my budget guide reflect the real cost of that standard in the Portland and Hood River markets.

Think of your wedding like a symphony. Your coordinator is the conductor, but the musicians still have to show up and know what they’re doing for the show to go on. No amount of coordination can compensate for a vendor who is underqualified, understaffed, or unreliable.

If those numbers feel out of reach right now, that’s okay. It just means the timing might not be right yet, and I’d rather you know that now than after you’ve booked. If you’re close but have questions, reach out. I’m happy to talk it through.

Pricing & Deposits

Planning packages start at $3,800 for a Reception Only celebration and $4,500 for Ceremony + Reception. Pricing is published on the website by celebration type and guest count. No inquiry required to see what it costs. Decor design is included in every package. The decor itself is priced separately, scales by guest count, and is invoiced directly by our rental partner.

The $1,500 deposit reserves your planning package, locks in your spot on my calendar, and is credited toward your planning package total. Your decor is invoiced separately by our rental partner.

Your planning fee is paid in three installments: the $1,500 deposit at booking, a second payment at the midpoint of your planning, and the final payment one week before your wedding date. The remaining balance after your deposit is split evenly between the second and final payments, and the final payment reflects your confirmed guest count.

Your decor is billed directly by our rental partner in two parts: a deposit when your initial rental order is placed, and the final balance about two weeks before your wedding, once the order is finalized. Planning payments are processed through Square; cash and checks aren’t accepted.

Guest counts can fluctuate within your booked tier at no additional charge. If your final count exceeds the Petite or Signature maximum by more than 15 guests, your planning package upgrades to the next tier and the price adjusts accordingly. If your count falls more than 15 below your tier minimum, you can move down one tier, up until you sign your venue contract.

Above 120 guests, pricing continues from the Grand package rather than stopping. You add a charge for each table beyond 15, plus a third onsite coordinator, and you can see the total on the reserve page before you book. 200 guests is the largest celebration I plan.

Decor collections are always invoiced based on your final confirmed guest count, so you only pay for the tables you actually need.

No, on both counts. Pricing is published before you reach out, and I never accept commissions or kickbacks from vendors. Every recommendation I make is based on what’s right for your wedding, not what benefits me financially.

Cancellations are submitted in writing to hello@higherloveeventco.com. The $1,500 deposit is non-refundable. Beyond that, what you owe depends on timing: more than 90 days before your wedding, the remaining balance isn’t charged; within 90 days, 50% of the remaining balance is due; within 30 days, the full remaining balance is due.

If you need to move your date, send the request in writing and I’ll check my availability. If I’m available, planning work already completed (venue research, vendor outreach, contract management, and timeline work) is invoiced before work on the new date begins. If I’m not available for the new date, the deposit is forfeited and the agreement ends.

Matching templates for your wedding website and digital save the dates and invites are also available if you’d like to use them.

Specific items can occasionally be unavailable. When that happens, I source a comparable replacement at a similar cost that keeps the look and quality of your collection.

The Wedding Day

Two, or three for celebrations above 120 guests. All are on-site for the full event, up to 8 hours for Reception Only packages and up to 12 hours for Ceremony + Reception packages, from the start of setup through the end of teardown. If the day runs past your coverage window, additional hours are billed at $65/hour, and whenever possible I’ll let you know before any extra time is added.

We oversee setup from start to finish: directing vendors, managing the production timeline, and making sure everything is executed according to plan. We style your tables and coordinate the placement of personal items like signage, guest books, and escort cards. The physical labor of setup (furniture, AV, catering equipment, and bar setup with alcohol you’ve purchased) is done by your individual vendors. Our job is to make sure every vendor knows exactly what to do and when, so the day comes together seamlessly.

We arrive before your guests, oversee vendor setup, run the ceremony and reception timeline, manage any issues that come up behind the scenes, and stay until the last vendor is out. You won’t be fielding questions or watching the clock, and neither will your family. Everyone who loves you gets to show up and celebrate you, not work your wedding.

It’s our job to anticipate problems before they happen and solve them before you know they exist. Between two coordinators on-site and a detailed production plan built well in advance, most issues are resolved without the couple ever knowing. If something truly unexpected happens, we take care of it. That’s what we’re there for.

Two things. For events longer than five hours, please include vendor meals for me and my coordinators, served at the same time as your guests, so we can stay sharp through the last dance. And if your venue charges for parking, those fees are covered by you for the rehearsal and wedding day.
