Choose your decor collection.

A decor collection is everything that dresses each dinner table: linen, place setting, glassware, candles, and florals. Eight complete looks, sourced, delivered, styled, and cleared. Choose one now if you already know the look you want, or decide later in planning once your venue and vendor team are set.
