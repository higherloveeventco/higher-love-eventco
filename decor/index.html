Wedding Decor

Portland Wedding Decor.
No design fee.

Decor is one of the most time-consuming and overwhelming parts of wedding planning. At Higher Love, I’ve taken it off your plate: eight collections, pre-designed and priced per table, so you know exactly what you’re getting and what it costs before you book.

A standalone wedding designer in Portland typically runs $1,500 to $5,000 or more. Because every collection here is already designed, there is no design fee. You pay for the decor itself, and nothing for the design on top.

How decor works.

01

Choose your collection

Browse the eight pre-designed collections and choose the one that fits your wedding. Select it at booking if you already know the look you want, or later in planning once your venue and vendor team are set and the aesthetic comes into focus.

02

Priced by your guest count

Collections are priced per table and invoiced separately by our rental partner, based on your confirmed guest count. Clear due dates, so nothing sneaks up on you.

03

I take it from there

Your tables are dressed before guests arrive and cleared after they leave. You never lift a box, coordinate a delivery, or think about a single place setting. You just arrive, and everything is ready.

Every collection includes

What’s on every table.

Every one of the eight collections is built around the same foundation. What changes is the aesthetic: the colors, textures, and finishing details that make each look its own.

The table

  • Full-length table linen
  • Coordinating runner
  • Coordinating napkins
  • Table number frame

Each place setting

  • Charger plate
  • Dinner plate
  • Water goblet
  • Dinner fork and knife

The centerpiece

  • 6 bud vases with florals
  • 6 votive candles
  • Taper or pillar candles, matched to the collection

Larger floral arrangements for the head table and welcome table are available as an add-on.

Included with every collection

A cohesive look, from the very beginning.

Your collection does not stop at the table. Every one comes with a matching suite of digital templates, so your save the dates, website, and signage all share the same look as your decor. Digital means no printing, no proofs, and no waiting on the mail. It is the lower-waste, lower-effort way to keep everything cohesive, with nothing left for you to source or coordinate.

Save the dates and invites

  • Digital save the date template
  • Digital invitation template
  • Matched to your collection

Wedding website

  • Ready-to-use website template
  • Designed to match your collection
  • Everything your guests need in one place

Signage suite

  • Welcome sign
  • Seating chart
  • Table numbers
  • Bar and catering menus
