The staircase closure, and what it means for 2027

When people picture a Cathedral Park wedding, they are almost always picturing the stone staircase directly beneath the bridge arches. It is the image that sells the place. It has also been unavailable for permitted weddings since October 1, 2022, while ODOT repairs the concrete on the pier columns, and that has been a disappointment for a lot of couples.

Many of us expected wedding permits for the staircase to return in 2027. They are not. Portland Parks’ 2027 wedding brochure lists the staircase and deck as unavailable for the 2027 season, so it cannot be reserved for a wedding with guests this year. I know that is hard news if you have been waiting for it, and I was surprised by it too.

Here is what is still possible. A simple ceremony of just the two of you, an officiant, and two witnesses can use the staircase on a first-come, first-served basis, as bridge repair work allows. And the Cathedral Park Amphitheater, just below the bridge, is available to reserve for weddings of any size up to 500 guests.

If you have your heart set on the staircase, you still have good options. You can elope on the staircase with a simple ceremony, reserve the amphitheater for a full wedding with the arches above you, or book Married Under the Bridge for an all-inclusive celebration in the park. And if you would rather wait for the staircase itself, join my staircase waitlist and I will let you know as soon as Portland Parks makes it reservable again.

The Cathedral Park Amphitheater

The amphitheater sits along N. Pittsburg Avenue, just below the St. Johns Bridge, with a stage and natural amphitheater seating that holds up to 500 people. It has been available through Portland Parks the entire time, and for 2027 it is the park’s reservable wedding venue for both ceremonies and receptions. You still get the arches as your backdrop. One thing to know: Portland Parks lists it as not ADA accessible, so if anyone in your group uses a wheelchair or finds uneven ground difficult, call them at 503-823-2525 to talk through access points.

Reserving Cathedral Park in 2027

Portland Parks opens wedding reservations in early January for that year’s dates, and for the popular venues it is a genuine line-up. People show up in person and wait.

For weddings between April 1 and December 31, 2027, in-person reservations open at 8:00 AM on Monday, January 4, 2027, at the Portland Building, 1120 SW 5th Avenue. Phone, online, and mail requests begin the next day. If your wedding falls between January and March 2027, you can skip the line and book now by phone. I wrote a full guide to Portland Parks Wedding Reservation Day, including what to bring and when to arrive.

Here are the practical details for the amphitheater that are easy to miss:

  • The amphitheater costs $740.75, and there is a non-refundable application fee on top of that. It is $133.50 if you book more than 45 days ahead, which brings the total to $874.25, plus a $275 refundable deposit. The application fee climbs to $200.25 inside 45 days and $266.75 inside 15, so booking early genuinely saves you money.
  • Electricity requires a Z75 key with a $110 refundable deposit, and it is a single outlet. I would plan on battery backup rather than relying on it.
  • DJs, bands, and amplified sound need a separate noise variance, and sound is limited to three consecutive hours between 11:00 AM and 8:00 PM.
  • Portable restrooms are worth the money, because the permanent ones are a walk away.
  • Proof of insurance is required if you are serving alcohol (beer and wine only) or if 50 or more people attend.
  • Dates in January, February, and March can be booked by phone now, so an early-in-the-year wedding lets you skip the queue entirely.

All of these fees are from Portland Parks’ 2027 wedding brochure, and they do change, so please confirm the current numbers with them directly at portland.gov/parks/wedding.

About the park

Cathedral Park sits in St. Johns, North Portland, directly under the eastern end of the St. Johns Bridge. The bridge opened in 1931, designed by David B. Steinman, and at 400 feet it is still Portland’s tallest and the only suspension bridge in the Willamette Valley. The gothic arched towers give the park its name.

It is twenty-one acres of riverfront, free to visit and open year-round. The park faces west, so late afternoon is when the light is at its best, and that is the window I would build a ceremony around. The bridge also provides partial rain cover, which makes Cathedral Park more forgiving than most outdoor ceremony spots in Portland.

Common questions

Cathedral Park wedding FAQ

Do you need a permit to get married at Cathedral Park?

It depends on where in the park you marry and how many people are with you. Portland Parks requires a permit for any legal ceremony in a park when guests are invited, meaning more than the two of you, an officiant, and two witnesses. For 2027, the amphitheater is the park’s reservable wedding venue; the staircase and deck is unavailable for permits through the 2027 season. A simple ceremony of the couple, an officiant, and two witnesses needs no permit at all and can use the staircase first-come, first-served as repair work allows. A reception with guests and food always needs its own separate reservation, whichever spot you choose.

When do Cathedral Park wedding permits open for 2027?

For weddings between April 1 and December 31, 2027, in-person reservations open at 8:00 AM on Monday, January 4, 2027, at the Portland Building, 1120 SW 5th Avenue. Phone, online, and mail requests begin Tuesday, January 5. If your date falls between January and March 2027, you can book now by phone at 503-823-2525 and skip the queue. My Reservation Day guide covers what to bring and when to arrive.

What’s the capacity at Cathedral Park?

The amphitheater holds up to 500 people. The staircase and deck holds up to 100 when it is reservable, but it is unavailable for permits through the 2027 season. If you are marrying without a permit anywhere in the park, the ceremony is limited to five people: the two of you, your officiant, and two witnesses.

Which is the better Cathedral Park ceremony spot?

For 2027, the amphitheater is the only Cathedral Park location you can reserve for a wedding with guests, and you still get the arches as your backdrop. The staircase is the iconic one, but this year it can only host a simple ceremony of five people, first-come, first-served, as repair work allows. If a guaranteed location and having your people present matter more to you than the staircase itself, I would take a reserved spot without hesitating.
