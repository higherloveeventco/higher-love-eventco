About Cathedral Park as a Cathedral Park wedding venue

Cathedral Park sits in the St. Johns neighborhood of North Portland, directly under the eastern end of the St. Johns Bridge. The bridge was designed by David B. Steinman and first opened in 1931. At 400 feet tall, it remains Portland's tallest bridge and the only suspension bridge in the Willamette Valley. The gothic arched towers give the park its name. Stand under them and you will immediately understand why couples keep coming back.

The park covers 21 acres along the riverfront and includes:

  • The iconic stairs and deck under the bridge arches, the ceremony spot couples dream about
  • A 500-person capacity amphitheater with a performance stage
  • Riverfront access with a boat dock and beach area
  • Paved accessible paths and accessible restrooms
  • Picnic areas, an off-leash dog area, and a nature patch garden completed in 2024

The best light for photos is late afternoon or early evening before sunset. The park faces west, so golden hour hits beautifully. The bridge also provides partial rain cover, which makes Cathedral Park a more weather-resilient option than most outdoor ceremony spots in Portland.

What is happening right now: the Cathedral Park bridge repair project

Here is the part most couples do not know until they have already fallen for this spot.

Cathedral Park and the St. Johns Bridge · Portland, OR

The Cathedral Park staircase and deck, the iconic ceremony spot under the arches, has been unavailable for permitted weddings since October 1, 2022. ODOT has been repairing the concrete on the pier columns and arches under the bridge, and the work has kept the staircase off-limits for permitted events ever since. The amphitheater stayed available the whole time, and small elopement ceremonies have continued at the staircase on a first-come, first-served basis, as repair work allows.

That changes in 2027. Full wedding permits return to Cathedral Park next year, for the first time in about five years.

ODOT is working on the piers near N. Crawford St. through December 2026, with site restoration in the park continuing into 2027. So the park will still be finding its way back to normal while the first permitted weddings in five years are happening in it. That is worth knowing if you are planning early in the year.

This is significant. There are roughly five years of pent-up demand for this spot, and reservations for 2027 dates open on a single morning in January. Couples who have been waiting are going to move fast. If you are planning a 2027 wedding at Cathedral Park, now is the time to start making moves. And if you are getting married sooner than that, keep reading, because there is a way to marry under the bridge this year.

Cathedral Park wedding permits: what to expect in 2027

Portland Parks and Recreation opens wedding permit reservations in early January each year for that year's dates. For popular venues like Cathedral Park, it is a literal line-up event.

Here is how 2026 ran, which is the best guide we have to how 2027 will work. In-person reservations for April through December dates were held on Monday, January 5 at 1120 SW 5th Avenue. Phone, online, and mail requests opened at 8 AM the following morning. Couples and planners line up before the doors open, because by the second day many Saturdays are already gone.

Key logistics to know:

  • Permit requests must be made at least 45 days in advance to avoid rush fees
  • Pre-closure pricing for the staircase and deck was $621.50 for up to 100 guests, subject to change for 2027
  • A key is required for electricity access, with a $110 refundable deposit, and the access point is about 150 feet from the ceremony space
  • Portable restrooms are strongly recommended, as the park's permanent restrooms are lower in the park and not close to the stairs
  • Liability insurance may be required for your permit
  • The staircase and deck are not ADA accessible
  • Dates falling in the first quarter of a year can usually be booked by phone ahead of the January rush, so a January, February, or March wedding avoids reservation day entirely

Cathedral Park wedding FAQ

Can you get married at Cathedral Park in Portland?

Yes. Cathedral Park is a permitted wedding venue managed by Portland Parks and Recreation. The staircase and deck under the St. Johns Bridge arches are the most requested spot, and they have been unavailable for permitted weddings since October 2022 during bridge repair work. Full wedding permits return in 2027. The amphitheater stayed available throughout, and small elopement ceremonies are allowed at the staircase on a first-come, first-served basis.

Can you get married under the St. Johns Bridge in 2026?

Yes, as an elopement. Small ceremonies are allowed at the staircase on a first-come, first-served basis while full wedding permits are unavailable. Married Under the Bridge is built for exactly this: a legal ceremony under the bridge, portraits in the park, then a styled picnic reception where your guests join you at 4:30. All-inclusive from $2,500.

How much does a Cathedral Park elopement cost?

Each price is all-inclusive: legal ceremony, officiant, license filing, photography, florals, grazing spread, desserts, beverages, permit fees, and all setup and styling. How much does a Cathedral Park wedding permit cost?

Pre-closure pricing for the staircase and deck was $621.50 for up to 100 guests. Permit pricing is set by Portland Parks and Recreation and is subject to change. Permit requests must be made at least 45 days in advance to avoid rush fees. Confirm current pricing directly with PP&R at portland.gov/parks/wedding or by calling 503-823-2525.

When do Cathedral Park wedding permits open?

Portland Parks and Recreation opens wedding permit reservations in early January each year. For 2026, in-person reservations were held on Monday, January 5 at 1120 SW 5th Avenue, with phone, online, and mail requests opening at 8 AM the next morning. For popular spots like Cathedral Park, couples and planners line up in person, because many Saturdays are gone by the second day. Dates in January through March can usually be booked by phone ahead of the rush.

Is the Cathedral Park staircase open for weddings in 2026?

No. The staircase and deck have been unavailable for permitted weddings since October 1, 2022 during ODOT concrete repair work on the St. Johns Bridge pier columns. Full wedding permits return in 2027, the first time in about five years. Reservations for 2027 dates open in early January 2027, and PP&R publishes each year's park unavailability calendar in January of that year.

What is the capacity at Cathedral Park for a wedding?

The staircase and deck accommodate up to 100 guests. The Cathedral Park Amphitheater accommodates up to 500 guests. An elopement ceremony at the staircase is limited to the couple, an officiant, and two witnesses.

Do you need a permit to get married at Cathedral Park?

It depends on the size of your ceremony. Portland city code requires a permit for a legal union ceremony in a public park where guests are invited. A simple ceremony of the couple, an officiant, and two witnesses does not require one. Any ceremony with guests, chairs, vendors, or amplified sound does, and a reception with guests and food needs its own separate reservation. Confirm the details for your ceremony with PP&R at portland.gov/parks/wedding.
