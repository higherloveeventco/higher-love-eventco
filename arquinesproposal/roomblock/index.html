Wedding Room Block

Michelle & Daniel · July 17, 2027

The short version
  • A room block holds hotel rooms for your guests at a discounted rate. Guests book and pay on their own.
  • You pay nothing up front. But if you hold too many rooms and they don't get reserved, you will be required to pay for the leftover held rooms.
  • So we start small and add more later, as rooms fill up.
One

What it actually is

The Jupiter sets aside a group of rooms under your names.

Your guests book those rooms at a rate lower than the public price, and they pay for their own stay. You are not buying the rooms.

Jupiter charges you nothing up front. After your guests check out on Sunday, July 18, Jupiter counts how many of your held rooms were actually reserved. If your guests reserved enough of them, you owe nothing. If they came up short, Jupiter charges you for the leftover rooms.

Two

The one risk

80% of rooms you hold must be reserved

If your guests reserve fewer than that, you pay out of pocket for the unused held rooms. Here is what that looks like.

Hold20 rooms
Need16 reserved
If only10 get reserved, you pay for 6 unused rooms

The starting number is what protects you. too few rooms costs you nothing. Holding too many results in an additional bill from the Jupiter.

Two things keep it manageable. Jupiter lets us release rooms we no longer need, and they send me reports as guests reserve, so I will see early if the number needs adjusting.

Three

What I'd suggest

Hold Friday July 16 through Sunday July 18. That covers the welcome reception and gives people a slow morning after.

Lean toward Jupiter Original. Over two nights it saves a guest about $160. Cheaper rooms mean more guests reserve inside the block, which keeps you clear of the 80%.

Start around 20 rooms. We add more the moment it fills up.

Five

How your guests find out

Your save the dates will be digital, and they'll link to your wedding website. The room block info lives on that website, with the rates and a booking link.

September

We set up your wedding website and begin uploading information, photos, and everything else guests will need. Once the room block is finalized and the website is ready, we send out your save the dates with a link to your wedding website and room block info.

Three weeks before the wedding

Any unreserved rooms go back to the hotel for general sale. Your wedding invitations will list a booking deadline well before that. Note: even after that release date, Jupiter will still honor your group rate for any rooms that happen to be open.
